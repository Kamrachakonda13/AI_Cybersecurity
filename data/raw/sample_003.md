# Data Retention and Deletion

User data is retained for 7 years after account closure to comply with financial regulations in the jurisdictions where we operate. This includes transaction records, communication logs, and identity verification documents.

Operational log data is retained for 90 days. Security audit logs are retained for 1 year. Both are stored separately from user data and are not subject to user deletion requests.

Backups are encrypted at rest using AES-256 with keys managed by the cloud provider's KMS. Backup retention is 35 days for daily snapshots and 12 months for monthly snapshots.

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days of verification. Deletion is irreversible; users must confirm in writing before the deletion workflow begins.

Data subject access requests (DSARs) are fulfilled within 30 days as required by GDPR. Extensions to 90 days are permitted for complex requests with written justification.
