# Customer Churn Analysis Report

## Overview

The churn report identifies at-risk customer accounts and analyzes reasons
for cancellation over the past 24 months.

## Datasets

- **Customer_Accounts** (fact): account lifecycle and status changes
- **Support_Tickets** (fact): ticket volume and resolution times
- **Product_Usage** (fact): feature adoption and engagement metrics

## Key metrics

- Monthly churn rate
- Customer lifetime value (CLV)
- Net Promoter Score (NPS) trend
- Support ticket volume per account

## Refresh schedule

Refreshes daily at 6 AM UTC.
