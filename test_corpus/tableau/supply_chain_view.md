# Supply Chain Operations View

## Overview

This workbook tracks inventory levels, supplier performance, and logistics
across the global supply chain.

## Data sources

- **Inventory_Snapshots** (extract): daily inventory by SKU and warehouse
- **Supplier_Orders** (live): purchase orders and delivery status
- **Logistics_Events** (extract): shipment tracking and lead times

## Key metrics

- Inventory turnover ratio
- On-time delivery rate
- Supplier defect rate
- Stockout frequency

## Refresh schedule

Extract refreshes every 6 hours.
