# HR Headcount Analysis

## Overview

The headcount workbook tracks organizational structure, hiring, attrition,
and compensation metrics.

## Data sources

- **Employee_Roster** (extract): current employees and org structure
- **Hiring_Pipeline** (live): open positions and candidates
- **Attrition_History** (extract): departures over the past 3 years

## Key metrics

- Headcount by department
- Voluntary attrition rate
- Time to fill open positions
- Compensation band distribution

## Refresh schedule

Extract refreshes daily at 3 AM UTC.
