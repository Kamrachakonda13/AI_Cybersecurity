# Product Requirements: Enterprise Search

## Overview

Build a unified search experience across all company knowledge sources.

## Goals

- Search across Confluence, SharePoint, Google Drive, and Slack
- Respect per-source permissions
- Return results in under 500ms
- Support natural language queries

## Out of scope

- Searching email
- Real-time document editing
- Cross-tenant search

## Success metrics

- Search adoption rate above 60% of active employees
- Time-to-answer reduced by 40%
- Zero unauthorized access incidents
