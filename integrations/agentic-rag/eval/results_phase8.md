# Phase 8 Results — AI-Powered SIEM Alert Triage

**Date:** 2026-09-21
**Feed:** 30 correlated incidents from 200 synthetic alerts
**Model:** openai/gpt-oss-20b via Groq
**Throttle:** 15 s between calls (free tier)

## Benchmark

|----------------|-----------|------------|

## Missed true positives

1 true-positive incidents were suppressed:

- `i-0020` (size=1, sev=MEDIUM): Single medium‑severity unsigned binary write to system32, no high‑severity alerts; likely benign or false positive.

## False positives incorrectly escalated

5 false-positive incidents were escalated:

- `i-0001` (size=8, sev=CRITICAL): Multiple high/critical alerts (root access, macro, C2, encoded PowerShell) indicate likely compromise.
- `i-0011` (size=1, sev=MEDIUM): [severity floor] MEDIUM incident; LLM suppress confidence 0.70 below floor 0.75. Original: Single medium-level data exfil alert without corroborating high-severity events; likely benign or false posit
- `i-0024` (size=1, sev=MEDIUM): [severity floor] MEDIUM incident; LLM suppress confidence 0.70 below floor 0.75. Original: Only one medium‑severity IDP alert from a service account; no high/critical alerts or clear malicious pattern
- `i-0032` (size=7, sev=CRITICAL): Multiple high/critical alerts show privilege removal, data exfiltration, and C2 activity, indicating real attacker behavior.
- `i-0037` (size=1, sev=CRITICAL): Critical alert shows MFA disabled for a privileged account, indicating a likely compromise that requires immediate investigation.

## Known limitations

1. Synthetic feed — models a SOC but does not replace a real one
2. Ground truth generated by the same tool that made the alerts
3. Throttled to 15 s/call due to free-tier quota
4. Grounding is on rule names + raw fields, not on a runbook corpus yet
5. No temporal chain correlation (multi-stage attacks that span >10 min)

## What Phase 8 demonstrates

- **The RAG engine generalizes.** Same audit, metrics, and LLM primitives
  used in Phases 2–7 now triage alerts.
- **Correlation is the multiplier.** 200 alerts → 122 incidents means
  triage reasons about units of work, not raw noise.
- **Fail-safe by default.** LLM unreachable → escalate with 0.0
  confidence. Never silently suppress.
- **Business impact is measurable.** Escalations removed, hours saved,
  recall held above a documented floor.
