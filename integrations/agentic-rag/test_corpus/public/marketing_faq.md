# Acme Platform - Marketing FAQ

## What is Acme?

Acme is a multi-tenant SaaS platform for enterprise workflow automation.
It helps teams design, deploy, and monitor automated business processes
without writing code.

## Who uses Acme?

Our customers range from small startups to Fortune 500 enterprises.
Common use cases include employee onboarding, invoice processing, and
customer support ticket routing.

## How is Acme priced?

Pricing is per active workflow, billed monthly. Volume discounts are
available for enterprise agreements. Contact sales for a quote.

## Where is Acme hosted?

Acme is hosted on AWS in US-East-1 and EU-West-1 regions. Data residency
options are available for regulated industries.
