# Incident Response Playbook

## Severity levels

- **P1**: Customer-facing outage. Page on-call immediately.
- **P2**: Degraded service. Slack notification, 30-minute SLA.
- **P3**: Minor issue. Ticket for next business day.

## Response flow

1. Acknowledge the alert.
2. Assign an incident commander.
3. Open a dedicated Slack channel.
4. Document timeline in real time.
5. Post-mortem within 5 business days.
