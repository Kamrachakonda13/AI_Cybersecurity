# Marketing Attribution Model

## Overview

This report attributes revenue to marketing channels using a multi-touch
attribution model.

## Datasets

- **Marketing_Events** (fact): ad impressions, clicks, conversions
- **Campaigns** (dimension): campaign names, budgets, channels
- **Customer_Journey** (fact): touchpoint sequence per customer

## Key metrics

- Cost per acquisition (CPA)
- Return on ad spend (ROAS)
- Channel-level conversion rate
- First-touch vs last-touch revenue

## Refresh schedule

Refreshes hourly.
